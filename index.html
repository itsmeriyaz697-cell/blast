    How to play

    Choose 1–8 players and a dare category. On each turn, the current player receives a challenge. If they complete it, tap Completed to award 100 points. Use New Dare to skip a challenge.

    👤 SoloPlay alone and try to beat your own score.
    👥 2–8 PlayersPass one device around and take turns.
    🎯 CategoriesFunny, acting, brain, home, quick and family-safe dares.

    About Qevrulix Dare

    Qevrulix Dare is a lightweight browser party game designed for phones, tablets and computers. No account is required to start playing. It includes 200 safe, fun dares across multiple categories.
