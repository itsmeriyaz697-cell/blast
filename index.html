How to play

Choose 1–8 players and a dare category. On each turn, the current player receives a challenge. If they complete it, tap Completed to award 100 points. Use New Dare to skip a challenge.

👤 Solo Play alone and try to beat your own score.
👥 2–8 Players Pass one device around and take turns.
🎯 Categories Funny, acting, brain, home, quick and family-safe dares.

About Qevrulix Dare

Qevrulix Dare is a lightweight browser party game designed for phones, tablets and computers. No account is required to start playing.
